# The Core Four: Customer Acquisition

## When to Use
- You need more customers and don't know where to find them
- You're dependent on a single channel and want to diversify
- You're overwhelmed by the 1,000 marketing tactics people recommend
- You want a simple, complete framework for all customer acquisition

## Fails When
- You haven't validated your offer; promoting a bad offer faster just burns money faster
- You try to launch all four channels simultaneously and do none of them well
- You use this as a planning exercise and never actually execute
- You ignore the "volume" component; each channel requires massive output to work

## Core Concept

There are only four ways to get customers. That's it. Four. Everything else is a variant of these four. Every business that has ever existed has used some combination of these four methods. Once you understand this, marketing goes from overwhelming to simple.

**1. Warm Outreach.** Reaching out to people who already know you. Your friends, family, past clients, social media connections, email list, community members. These people already trust you to some degree, so the conversion rate is highest here. This is where every business should start because it costs nothing and converts the best.

**2. Cold Outreach.** Reaching out to people who don't know you. Cold email, cold DM, cold calling, LinkedIn outreach, direct mail. These people have no idea who you are, so conversion rates are lower but the volume is unlimited. This is the second channel to add because it's still low-cost and you control the volume.

**3. Content (Free).** Posting content that attracts people to you. Social media posts, YouTube videos, podcasts, blog articles, TikToks. This is "advertising" you don't pay for with money; you pay with time and effort. The leverage here is enormous because one piece of content can reach millions, but it takes time to build momentum. This is the long game.

**4. Paid Ads.** Paying platforms to show your message to targeted audiences. Facebook ads, Google ads, YouTube ads, LinkedIn ads, podcast ads. This is instant leverage; you trade money for attention. But it requires a proven offer and enough margin to afford the customer acquisition cost. This is the last channel to add because it amplifies what's already working.

Now here's the layer most people miss. Each of these four can be done in two ways: **"Do" and "Done-for-you."**

"Do" means you do it yourself. You personally reach out, create content, or run ads.
"Done-for-you" means you hire someone or build a system to do it. An affiliate sends you warm referrals. A cold-calling agency reaches out on your behalf. An influencer creates content about you. An ad agency runs your campaigns.

That gives you 8 total methods:

```
Channel          | Do (you)              | Done For You (others)
-----------------|-----------------------|-------------------------
Warm Outreach    | You reach out         | Referrals/Affiliates
Cold Outreach    | You cold call/email   | Agency/Sales team
Content (Free)   | You post content      | Influencer/Guest posts
Paid Ads         | You run ads           | Agency/Media buyer
```

**The progression.** Start with warm outreach (free, high conversion). Once you're getting consistent results, add cold outreach (free/cheap, unlimited volume). Once your offer is proven and you have cash flow, add content (free, long-term leverage). Once everything is profitable, add paid ads (scale what works). This is the order for most businesses. Warm, cold, content, paid.

**The math of each channel:**

Warm outreach: You have 500 connections. You reach out to 10 per day. 30% respond. 15% book a call. 25% buy. That's 10 x 0.30 x 0.15 x 0.25 = 0.11 clients per day, or about 3 per month. At $3,000 per client, that's $9,000/month from warm outreach alone. And it cost you $0.

Cold outreach: You send 100 cold emails per day. 5% respond. 20% of responders book a call. 15% buy. That's 100 x 0.05 x 0.20 x 0.15 = 0.15 clients per day, or about 4-5 per month. At $3,000, that's $12,000-15,000/month. Cost: essentially $0 plus time.

Content: You post daily for 6 months. You build to 10,000 followers. 1% engage meaningfully. 10% of engaged become leads. 10% of leads buy. That's 10,000 x 0.01 x 0.10 x 0.10 = 1 client per batch of content. But content compounds. A year in, you're getting 5-10 inbound leads per week without doing outreach. This is the compounding channel.

Paid ads: You spend $5,000/month. Your cost per lead is $25. That's 200 leads. 10% book calls. 25% buy. That's 200 x 0.10 x 0.25 = 5 clients. At $3,000, that's $15,000 revenue on $5,000 ad spend. 3:1 return. This scales linearly; spend $50,000, get $150,000.

Combine all four and you're at $50,000+/month. That's the power of the Core Four running simultaneously.

## How to Apply

1. **Start with warm outreach today.** Make a list of every person you know who could buy or refer someone. Not who you think will buy. Who COULD buy. Text, DM, or email 10 of them today. Not with a pitch. With a question: "Hey, I'm launching something new for [target]. Do you know anyone who [has the problem]?" This works because you're asking for referrals, not selling, and it doesn't feel pushy.

2. **Build your cold outreach system.** Pick one channel: cold email, cold DM, or cold calling. Build a list of 1,000 ideal prospects. Write a message that leads with value, not a pitch. "I noticed [specific observation about their business]. I put together [free resource] that might help. Want me to send it?" Send 50-100 per day. Track response rates. Iterate on the message weekly.

3. **Start creating content on one platform.** Pick the platform where your target audience spends time. Create one piece of content per day for 90 days. Don't worry about quality at first. Volume beats quality until you have enough reps to be both. Document what you're doing in your business, share frameworks, answer common questions, tell stories.

4. **Add paid ads only after the other three are profitable.** Take your best-performing content (the stuff that got the most engagement organically) and put money behind it. Start with $20-50/day. Let it run for 7 days. Measure cost per lead. If cost per lead is profitable based on your conversion rates, scale up. If not, test a different piece of content or angle.

5. **Activate "done-for-you" variants.** Once each channel is working with you doing it, systematize and delegate. Create a referral program for warm outreach. Hire a VA or agency for cold outreach. Partner with influencers for content. Hire a media buyer for ads. Now you have 8 channels running.

## Examples

**Situation:** A new business coach with no audience, no list, and no budget.

**Application:** Week 1-4: Warm outreach. Message 200 past colleagues and connections asking if they know any business owners struggling with [specific problem]. Book 15 discovery calls, close 3 at $2,000. Revenue: $6,000. Week 5-8: Cold outreach. Use LinkedIn to find 500 business owners in target niche. Send personalized connection requests and messages. Book 10 calls, close 2. Revenue: $4,000. Week 9-12: Start posting daily on LinkedIn about frameworks and client results. Begin building audience.

**Illustrative result:** $10,000 in revenue in 8 weeks with zero ad spend. Content starts compounding. By month 6, inbound leads from content reduce the need for outreach.

**Situation:** An established SaaS company doing $50k/month from paid ads only. Worried about ad platform dependency.

**Application:** Keep paid ads running. Add cold email campaign targeting ideal customer profile (1,000 emails/week). Start a YouTube channel with weekly tutorials and case studies. Launch a customer referral program offering one month free for each referral.

**Illustrative result:** Within 6 months: paid ads still at $50k. Cold email adds $15k. YouTube starts generating $5k in inbound. Referral program adds $10k. Total: $80k/month with significantly reduced dependency on any single channel.

## Anti-Patterns (tactical)

**Don't:** Try to launch all four channels in the first month.
**Why:** You'll do all of them at 25% effort and none will work. Each channel requires focused attention to get the flywheel spinning. Start with one. Master it. Then add the next. One channel done at 100% beats four channels done at 25%.

**Don't:** Skip warm outreach because "I don't want to bother my friends."
**Why:** Your friends want you to succeed. And you're not pitching them; you're asking if they know someone who needs help. This is the fastest, cheapest, highest-converting channel. Skipping it because of ego is leaving easy money and early validation on the table.

**Don't:** Run paid ads before your offer converts organically.
**Why:** Paid ads amplify what's already working. If your offer doesn't convert from warm outreach (free), it won't convert from paid ads (expensive). You'll just lose money faster. Prove the offer works with free channels first.

**Don't:** Do cold outreach with a generic, spammy message.
**Why:** "Hi, I help businesses grow" sent to 10,000 people will get you flagged as spam and generate zero clients. Cold outreach requires personalization, specificity, and leading with value. Quality per message matters more than total volume of garbage messages.

**Don't:** Measure content success by views or followers.
**Why:** Views don't pay bills. Revenue pays bills. Track how many leads your content generates, how many of those leads convert, and what the revenue per piece of content is. A video with 500 views that generates one $5,000 client is worth more than a video with 500,000 views that generates zero.
